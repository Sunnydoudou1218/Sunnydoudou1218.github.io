Research

Spillovers from public-welfare videos on Bilibili

Undergraduate thesis · 2026

A full year of creator data, 276 propensity-matched pairs, two-way fixed effects, robust to a Callaway–Sant'Anna staggered difference-in-differences. Engagement on a creator's later non-welfare videos rose; sentiment moved the same way but rarely reached significance. The gain sat with the creators who had been less active.

Churn prediction, interpretable end to end

16,000 customer records cut to 27 variables. Test accuracy 78.1% from a logistic baseline, 82.1% with a hinge loss, then 95.4% with an EBM, every variable still readable.

AI companions and self-disclosure

I built the agent on Coze; our team's paper on how output modality shifts perceived social presence appeared at CNAIS 2025.

Detecting machine-written text on a compute budget

830,000 texts. A fine-tuned TF-IDF SVM reached 92% accuracy and 95% recall, ahead of a three-layer LSTM and a CNN under the same budget.
